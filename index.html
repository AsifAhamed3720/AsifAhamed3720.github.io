Flagship project

Smart Student Blueprint

Live in production Built solo Made for Bangladesh

A complete online school, from the first lesson to the final grade.

Most course sites stop at the video. Smart Student Blueprint takes a learner the whole way: finding a course, paying with the mobile wallet already on their phone, learning at their own pace, then sitting a full-length IELTS test that looks and behaves like the real computer-based exam, with their band score worked out the moment they submit.

Teachers get everything needed to run it, from writing the exam to confirming payments and watching revenue. I designed, built and run it myself, since May 2026.

Intelligent Budgeted Web Crawler

Active Embedding-Based Adaptation · AI Systems Project

February 2025 - April 2025

AI/ML

An AI-powered web crawler that seeks out the most relevant pages on a topic while staying within a fixed budget of pages to visit. Rather than blindly following every link, it uses Sentence-BERT language embeddings to judge how relevant each page and link is, prioritizes the most promising ones, and keeps learning as it crawls to sharpen its judgment. Across 25 test runs it averaged 95% precision, consistently outperforming standard breadth-first and keyword-based crawlers.
